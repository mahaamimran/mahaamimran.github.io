03 · The UX half

I design what I ship.

I'm the designer, the engineer, and the analyst. I watch what people actually tap in Firebase, then decide what to build, simplify, or cut.

  1. Watch the taps. Analytics shows where attention goes and where it dies.
  2. Make the call. Build it, simplify it, or cut it. No committee.
  3. Ship and re-measure. The next release answers whether I was right.

The tap overlay on the phone is illustrative.

06 · Reliability

Crash-free isn't an accident.

Widget lifecycle bugs, layout overflows, dispose-after-unmount errors. I trace them in Crashlytics and fix them, release after release. Qur'an for All sits at 97.8% crash-free on Android and 98.5% on iOS, with 6.1 sessions per user.

07 · The release train

Ship it. Calmly.

GitHub Actions, PR quality gates, Fastlane Match, TestFlight and Play beta, then staged rollouts with force-update. Releases are boring on purpose.
